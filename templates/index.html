About This Database

EndolysinDB catalogues endolysins and phage lytic proteins with experimentally verified antibacterial activities.

Data is sourced from UniProt, InterPro, PDB, and published research papers. All records distinguish experimental evidence from inferred or literature-reported information.

Domain annotations, activity data, and target organism records are populated from verified sources only. Empty tables indicate data not yet imported — not absence of activity.
